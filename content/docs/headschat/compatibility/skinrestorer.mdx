## SkinRestorer Compatibility

In the idea, HeadsChat have the compatibility layer to update the components when a player change their skin trough SkinRestorer.

However, you client is caching the texture for ~an hour, so you won't see the change immediately. 
I don't have any option to invalidate this cache, and I have no way to create a workaround, as the libs I use actually respect the privacy of MineSkin's contributors.
