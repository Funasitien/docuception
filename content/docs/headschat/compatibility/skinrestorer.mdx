## SkinRestorer Compatibility

In the idea, HeadsChat have the compatibility layer to update the components when a player change their skin trough SkinRestorer. The player profile is forcefully refetched thus updating the head icon.

Some Minecraft Client implements hard chache, where the method used by HeadChat to clear this said cache do not work.
I can't do anything about this at the moment.
